Research Purpose And Boundary

This part preserves the individual scalar attribution and correlation inventory for the frozen Task 43 raw-inclusive hybrid. The root reference owns the benchmark claim, component-head performance, family totals, mapping contrast, population stability, and external comparison. This part owns only the 37 scalar rows and their measured overlap on the fixed 5,042-row training matrix.

Mean absolute TreeSHAP measures use by the existing nonlinear ensemble. The Pearson values measure association. Neither quantity is an additive QWK contribution, causal effect, or feature-removal result.
